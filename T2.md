The Nila Coffee Co. Support Policy outlines various terms and conditions related to orders, shipping, returns,
refunds, and cancellations. Here’s a summary of the key points:

1. **Shipping Speed**:
   - Orders ship within 2 business days of payment.
   - Delivery time depends on the location:
     - 2-4 days for metro cities (Chennai, Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru).
     - 5-8 days for other locations.

2. **Shipping and Returns**:
   - Shipping is free for orders above Rs 699.
   - A flat Rs 59 shipping fee applies otherwise.
   - Unopened packs can be returned within 7 days of delivery.
   - Opened packs cannot be returned unless they were damaged or the wrong item was sent.

3. **Refunds**:
   - Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after the warehouse receives the return.
   - Refunds are made by the original payment method.
   - Cash-on-delivery orders are refunded by bank transfer.

4. **Order Cancellations**:
   - Orders can be cancelled before they ship.
   - Once shipped, an order cannot be cancelled.

5. **Monthly Subscriptions**:
   - Monthly subscriptions get 10% off.
   - Skip or cancel at least 48 hours before the next billing date.

6. **Support Hours**:
   - Support is available Monday to Saturday, 10am to 6pm IST.
   - Contact: support@nilacoffee.example

7. **Gift Cards**:
   - Gift cards are valid for 12 months and are non-refundable.

8. **Reporting Damaged Items**:
   - Send a photo of a damaged item within 48 hours of delivery.
